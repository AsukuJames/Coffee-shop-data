# Coffee-shop-data

### Dataset Overview 
Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard – An interactive dashboard tracking sales performance across stores, products, and time. Features KPIs (revenue, transactions, avg. sale), product/store comparisons, weekday sales trends, and time-band analysis, with slicers for dynamic filtering by category, date, and location.

### Dataset Descriptions 
the dataset contains monthly coffee shop sales information across different regions and categories. 

| Columns               | description                            |
|- - - - - - - - - - - -| - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -| 
|Transaction_ID         |Unique ID for each transaction          |
|Transaction_Date       |Date the transaction occurred           |
|Transaction_Time       |Time the transaction occurred           |
|Store_Location         |Store where the sale occurred           |
|Product_Category.      |Main category of the product            |
|Product_Type           |Type or group of the product            |
|Product_Name           |Name of the product sold                |
|Quantity               |Number of items sold                    |
|Unit_Price             |Price per individual item               |
|Total_Sales            |Total revenue from the transaction      |
|Day_Name               |Day of the week                         |
|Month                  |Month of the transaction                |
|Month_Number           |Numeric representation of the month     |
|Hour                   |Hour the transaction occurred           |
|Time_Band              |Period of the day for the sale          |
